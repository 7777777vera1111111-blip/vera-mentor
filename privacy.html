Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to this website (the “Site”) and to the private app “Vera Content Assistant” (the “App”), operated by Vera, a holistic mentor (“I”, “me”).

1. Use of the Pinterest API

The App uses the official Pinterest API to publish pins (an image, a title, a description and a link) to boards on my own Pinterest account. I connect my account through Pinterest’s standard OAuth sign-in and request only the permissions needed for this purpose.

2. Not affiliated with Pinterest

This Site and the App are independent. They are not endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with Pinterest. “Pinterest” is a trademark of Pinterest, Inc.

3. What data is handled and what happens to it

  • Access token. After I sign in, Pinterest gives the App an access token. It is stored privately on my own server and is used only to publish my pins and to read the names of my own boards. It is never shown publicly.
  • Pinterest-derived data. The App reads only basic information about my own account (for example, board names and the status of pins I published). This data is used only to operate the App for me. It is not shared with anyone, not published, not combined with other data sets and not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.
  • My own content. The images, titles and descriptions that I publish are my original materials.
  • Retention. I keep the access token and board information only while the App is in use. When I disconnect the App or delete my account data, they are deleted.

4. No resale or redistribution

I do not sell, license, resell or redistribute Pinterest content or any data derived from Pinterest to third parties. I do not use Pinterest data to train machine learning or AI models.

5. This website

The Site is informational. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies for tracking and no analytics. I do not collect personal information from visitors. If you contact me through Telegram, I only see what you choose to send and I use it only to reply to you.

6. Sharing

I do not share personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

7. Your choices

You may ask me to delete anything you sent me by contacting me on Telegram. Pinterest users can revoke the App’s access at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Account management → Permissions (or “Apps”).

8. Children

The Site and the App are not directed to children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page.
